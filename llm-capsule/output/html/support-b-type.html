Frequently Asked Questions

What is LLM Capsule?

LLM Capsule is a context-preserving data layer that lets you use LLMs without losing the context of sensitive data. Sensitive information in your sources and messages is de-identified before being sent to the LLM, while preserving structure and context to maintain response quality. De-identified data in responses can be restored to the original values for review.

Can I try it for free before deploying?

Yes. We offer a free trial. You can apply on the demo request page. After review, our team will send you account credentials and onboarding instructions within 24 hours.

How long is the trial period?

14 days from the date your account is issued. The countdown starts from the issue date regardless of when you first log in, so we recommend getting started as soon as you receive the onboarding email.

How is sensitive data protected?

Sensitive information in your uploaded sources and messages is de-identified before being sent to the LLM. As a result, no sensitive data ever leaves your environment.

How do I restore de-identified data?

Click the Restore View button at the bottom of the response message to see the original data.

What source files can I upload?

We support TXT, DOCX, CSV, XLSX, HWPX, and text-based PDF files. You can upload up to 10 files at a time, with a maximum of 10 MB per source. Image-based PDFs without a text layer can be uploaded after OCR processing.

Can I access admin features with a trial account?

Trial accounts have access to app features only. Admin features are available through a separate guided demo session. Contact us at contact@cubig.ai to request one.

What happens to my data when the trial ends?

Your account and all data are permanently deleted at the moment the trial expires and cannot be recovered. Please back up any results you need before your trial ends.

How does the full deployment process work?

Deployment requirements, environment setup, and timelines are coordinated with our sales team. Please contact us at contact@cubig.ai to get started.

I encountered an error during the free trial.

Please contact us at contact@cubig.ai. Including the time the error occurred and what you were doing will help us resolve it faster.
